Delete Your Handify Account

If you want to delete your Handify account and associated data, you can request account deletion by sending us an email.

How to request deletion

Click the button below to send an account deletion request. Please send the request from the email address associated with your Handify account.

Delete My Handify Account

This button will open your email app with a pre-filled deletion request.

What will be deleted?

After verifying your request, we will delete your Handify account and associated data, including:

  • Your name and account information
  • Your email address
  • Your generated content
  • Your saved handwriting and creation data
  • Other personal data associated with your Handify account

Data retention

We do not retain your personal account data after your deletion request has been processed, except where retention is required by law or for legitimate legal or security purposes.
